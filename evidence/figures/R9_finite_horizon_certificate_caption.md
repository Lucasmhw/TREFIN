Global sensitivity constants and uniform finite-horizon neighborhood bounds.** (a) Original and analytically tightened registered-domain sensitivity radii for all 15 frozen checkpoints; each connected pair is one fold-seed model. Analytic block bounds hold uniformly throughout each specified neighborhood; their finite products propagate initial errors bounded by one tenth of the radii. All 480 trajectories satisfy the containment criterion at every audited radius. The registered-domain bounds in (a) and neighborhood bounds in (b,c) have their respective stated domains.
