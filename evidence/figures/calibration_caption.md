**Figure R5. Coverage, sharpness, and distribution score under positive innovation scaling.** Points show means over five separately trained, frozen TREFIN models; error bars show sample standard deviations across seeds. The original validation-selected mean shrinkage and point forecasts are retained. Fixed validation ES selects one positive scale per model by validation energy score; fixed validation 90% selects it to attain pooled 90% validation interval coverage. Both use 256 validation scenarios and freeze the scale for test evaluation. Causal rolling calibration uses the 126 most recent completed forecast origins, pooling their marginal conformity scores; an origin enters the window once all five outcomes have been realized. This scale updates sequentially during test evaluation. All methods reuse the original 256 test scenarios. Coverage is pooled over origins, leads, and assets; width is expressed in percentage points of return. Lower energy score is better. The horizontal reference marks nominal 90% coverage.
