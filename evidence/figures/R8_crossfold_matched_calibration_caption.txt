**Figure R8. Matched mean calibration across three chronological test periods.** Each panel compares TREFIN with TEDM, Timer-XL, TimeXer and TimeMixer++ under the documented task adapters. Every method has five training seeds in each fold. Symbols show mean MSE or joint energy score before and after validation-only positive mean shrinkage; error bars are sample standard deviations across seeds. Training, normalization, checkpoint selection and calibration use only the corresponding fold’s training/validation periods. The frozen fitting and calibration protocol for the four adapters is shared across folds. All comparisons align the same test origins within each fold, 32 assets and five leads, with 256 test scenarios. TEDM supplies native stochastic scenarios, the three point backbones use centered joint training-residual paths, and TREFIN uses its original probabilistic decoder. Axis ranges vary by fold; before/after points connected by a line belong to the same fixed predictor. Narrow seed error bars may be smaller than their markers.
