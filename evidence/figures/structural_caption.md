**Figure R6. Matched structural comparisons on fold 1.** Fixed and validation-selected configurations are shown for all five variants, each trained with seeds 1, 7, 19, 37 and 73 and the same positive validation-only mean-calibration procedure. Panels (a–c,f) show five-seed means and sample SD. Concept MAE averages standardized errors over future leads 1–5 and all coordinates within each run; graph IoU averages future-lead IoU within each run. Seed SDs are calculated from these per-run averages. Graph support uses strict absolute off-diagonal weight >0.05; empty unions have IoU 0. Panels (d,e) show candidate-minus-Full loss differences and 95% paired circular-block-bootstrap intervals with 20-origin blocks. Each origin retains all assets and leads; matched seed losses are averaged before temporal resampling. Positive differences favor Full. NoGraph removes the A input, rollout and decoder channel while retaining the market state, including its residual-dependence summary.
